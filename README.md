# "Skye": a late 19th century font

All glyphs(*) hand-drawn from "The Skye Collection of the Best Reels and Strathspeys Extant". Edinburgh: Paterson & Sons, 1887.

(*) there are no instances of some shapes in the source above. The following glyphs are from other sources:
- whole notehead drawn from other publication of Paterson & Sons
- dynamic symbols for m r s z and n are from the LilyPond font "Beethoven"
- timeSig0-9 taken from "Beethoven", 2 and 4 are edited
- restQuarter drawn from a 1931 publication of Paterson & Sons

The glyhs set is not complete. However, the most distinguishing features are there...
The remaining glyphs (at least in MuseScore) will fall back on "Bravura".
